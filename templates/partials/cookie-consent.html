{# Cookie / storage consent sheet. Deliberately honest about scope: this project ships no analytics, no pixels and nothing third-party (grep for gtag/matomo/facebook -- there are no hits). So the only real choice on offer is whether the browser may keep archive pages for offline reading and remember where you left off. The copy says exactly that instead of implying tracking we do not do. Essential, and never gated: the Masonite session cookie and the CSRF token. The page cannot be served without them, so they are stated, not offered. The choice itself lives in localStorage under `presspoint.consent`, which is essential-tier storage by the same logic -- refusing to remember a refusal would re-prompt on every visit. #}

Storage on this device

We keep a session cookie so the page can load, and nothing else by default. There is no advertising or analytics tracking on this site.

What each option does
Essential always on
Session and security (CSRF) cookies. Required to serve the page.
Optional
Saves archive covers and pages on your device so issues open instantly and keep working offline, and remembers the year and category you were last browsing.
